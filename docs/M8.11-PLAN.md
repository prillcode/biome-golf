# M8.11 — Swing Meter Precision

**Status:** Implemented in 0.8.5. See `docs/M8.11-CLOSEOUT.md` for verification and the
remaining manual meter-feel boundary. Small, independently shipped bug-fix milestone.

This milestone fixes a discrete-timing defect in the three-click accuracy meter. It is
deliberately narrow: no club balance, lie, physics, scoring, or input-model changes.

## Purpose

A visually perfect accuracy click can never land dead center on the meter. The reported
symptom is that a "perfect" swing always reads left or right of center even when the
marker sits inside the drawn perfect zone.

Root cause is arithmetic, not feel:

- `SwingController.meterValue(ticks, halfSweepTicks)` samples `k / halfSweepTicks` on the
  rising half of a triangular wave.
- `ACCURACY_HALF_SWEEP_TICKS = 15` (odd), so the reachable values are `0, 1/15, ...,
  14/15, 1` and `0.5` is **never** a sample.
- The closest samples are `7/15 = 0.4667` and `8/15 = 0.5333`, i.e. a guaranteed minimum
  miss of `1/30` (`0.0333`).
- `ShotResolver` treats `0.5` as perfect and scales deviation by `|2a − 1|`, so the
  best-possible click always carries a small directional error.
- The HUD then draws the marker at `round(159 * value)`, so it lands at pixel `74` (6 px
  left of the 160-wide bar's center) or `85` (5 px right) — never centered, while the
  14 px perfect zone makes it *look* centered.

`POWER_HALF_SWEEP_TICKS = 24` is even, so power reaches exactly `0.5` and `1.0`; only the
accuracy lane is affected.

## Design Direction

Extract the meter math into a Minecraft-free, unit-tested model and make its perfect value
land exactly on the discrete grid.

1. **Shared meter model.** Add a small pure type (for example `SwingMeter` or
   `TriangularMeter`) in `pro.apdev.biomegolf.club` (or `golf`) that owns the triangular
   sweep and its constants. `SwingController` delegates to it instead of carrying a
   private `meterValue`. This makes the parity contract testable on the plain JVM.

2. **Reachable perfect.** Use an even half-sweep for accuracy (for example `16`) so the
   center sample is exactly `0.5`, or define the sweep in terms of an odd number of lane
   positions with an explicit center index. Either way, `PERFECT_ACCURACY` must be on the
   sample grid. Keep power's even `24` so `1.0` stays reachable.

3. **WYSIWYG perfect zone.** Tie the HUD's drawn perfect-zone width and the resolver's
   neutral band to one shared constant so "inside the green" means "no deviation."
   Recommended resolver model:

   ```text
   miss = max(0, (|2a − 1| − BAND) / (1 − BAND))
   ```

   with `a = 0.5` (and any sample within `BAND`) producing zero deviation, and a full miss
   (`a = 0` or `1`) still producing the existing `FULL_MISS_FAN_DEG = 14`. The current
   green zone is about `±0.044` in meter value; a `BAND` near `0.05` makes the drawn zone
   and the physics agree and gives roughly three consecutive perfect samples.

4. **HUD marker centering.** Draw the accuracy marker centered on its value rather than
   offset by `−1`, so a `0.5` sample renders centered in the bar.

The exact constants are tuning inputs, not locked contracts.

## Locked Contracts

- The server remains the only shot authority; the client still sends only intent
  (`power`, `accuracy`).
- `ShotResolver` clamps and resolves exactly as before; only the miss-to-fan mapping
  changes, and it stays deterministic and repeatable.
- Full-miss deviation magnitude is unchanged; only near-center behavior becomes exact.
- Power behavior is unchanged.
- No change to club definitions, surfaces, profiles, shot types, lie rules, scoring,
  ownership, or the round lifecycle.

## Bounded Slices

### S0 — Meter Model Extraction and Parity Tests

- Move the triangular sweep into a pure, Minecraft-free model with explicit constants.
- Add tests proving: endpoints `0` and `1` are reachable; the period and symmetry hold;
  the chosen accuracy sweep has `0.5` on the sample grid; power still reaches `1.0`.
- Reproduce the current bug as a failing test first, then fix it.
- Verify with `./gradlew test`.

### S1 — Resolver Neutral Band

- Add the shared perfect-band constant and the remapped miss function to `ShotResolver`.
- Tests: every club on `0.5` and inside the band yields exactly zero lateral deviation;
  just outside the band deviates monotonically; `a = 0`/`a = 1` still reach the full fan;
  sign (left/right) is unchanged.
- Preserve `ShotMeterTest` regression behavior.

### S2 — HUD Alignment and Client Wiring

- Point `SwingController` at the shared model.
- Center the accuracy marker; draw the perfect zone from the shared band so the visual
  zone and the resolver agree.
- Confirm no client class leaks into `src/main`.
- Verify Loom client startup and manual meter behavior.

### S3 — Playtest and Closeout

- Manual: repeated accuracy clicks land dead center when timed to the center; in-zone
  clicks fly straight; out-of-zone misses still fan left/right.
- Confirm no perceived regression in power timing or swing responsiveness.
- Record tuning and closeout.

## Non-Goals

- Club balance, driver/lie penalties, or the tee-box work in M8.12.
- Changes to the three-click input model, meter speed as a difficulty lever, or
  first-try/fixed-swing rules.
- New shot types, spin, wind, prediction, or camera changes.
- Any server payload or scoring change.

## Open Decisions

1. Fix mechanism: even accuracy half-sweep vs. an explicit lane-step model. (Both are
   equivalent; the lane model reads more honestly in code.)
2. Include the resolver neutral band? Recommended, because it makes the drawn green zone
   truthful. It does slightly increase forgiveness and should be confirmed by playtest.
3. `BAND` magnitude and whether the HUD zone should shrink to match a narrower band.

## Exit Criteria

M8.11 is complete when:

1. The accuracy meter's perfect value is exactly reachable, verified by unit test.
2. A dead-center click produces exactly zero lateral deviation for every club.
3. Clicks inside the drawn perfect zone produce zero deviation, and the drawn zone and the
   resolver share one constant.
4. Full misses still fan to the existing maximum, with unchanged left/right sign.
5. Power, clubs, profiles, scoring, ownership, and lifecycle behavior are unchanged.
6. Automated, Loom, Docker, installed-client, and manual meter verification passes.
