# M8.14 Closeout — Decked Driver Rollout

## Delivered

- Decked Standard Driver landing horizontal retention is raised to `0.40` (from the
  lofted-club baseline `0.25`) to add a modest runner after landing.
- Standard's existing rolling-friction multiplier is unchanged. This distinction matters:
  a lower rolling multiplier brakes faster; Stinger's `0.65` multiplier is not used for the
  new Standard runner.
- Decked Driver launch scaling and accuracy spread are unchanged. A selected Driver
  Stinger remains on the existing Stinger landing/rolling profile.
- Unit simulation verifies the decked Standard Driver travels farther than the former
  launch-only deck profile, remains shorter than a teed Driver, and remains shorter than a
  standard Fairway Wood. Tee shots and other clubs are unchanged.

## Units Correction

Club catalog carry values (`Driver 150`, `Fairway Wood 125`) are in **blocks**, not yards.
The HUD converts blocks to displayed yards at 1.75 yards per block. The deck Driver HUD's
75% carry estimate is 112.5 blocks (about 196.9 displayed yards); the Fairway Wood nominal
estimate is 125 blocks (218.75 displayed yards). These values are nominal estimates, not
simulated total ranges.
