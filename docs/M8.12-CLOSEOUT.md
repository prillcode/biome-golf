# M8.12 — Off-Tee Driver Discipline — Closeout

**Status:** Implemented and verified. Shipped in **0.8.5**. Manual tee-vs-deck
playtest on a par 5 remains (see *Remaining boundary*).

## Problem

A Driver hit from anywhere other than a tee behaved exactly like a teed Driver: the
only restriction was `DRIVER_NOT_ALLOWED_ON_SAND`, and no part of the shot path knew
whether the ball was on a tee or on the deck. Long par 5s were reachable with a free
full-power fairway bomb, so the Driver had no meaningful lie cost.

## What Changed

- Added `BallLie` (`TEE`, `DECK`) and `LieRules` — pure, tested lie classification
  and deck-penalty data.
- Added `ShotPhysicsProfile.withLaunchScaled(horizontal, vertical)`, used to compose
  the lie onto the selected shot-type profile (landing/rolling behavior is retained,
  so the lie stacks with Standard/Stinger/etc.).
- `ShotResolver` gained an `accuracySpreadMultiplier`; the reuse of `SwingMeter`'s
  band (M8.11) is shared here.
- `ShotService` derives the authoritative lie and applies the deck penalty:
  - tee anchors = the player's active-hole tee (same dimension) plus the practice-range
    tee (same dimension);
  - empty anchor list (unknown context) yields `TEE`, so the penalty is never applied
    without a tee reference;
  - `LieRules.penalizes` limits the penalty to the Driver.
- `HoleStatePayload` now carries a display-safe `BallLie lie`; payload id bumped
  `hole_state_v6` → `hole_state_v7` (server and client must match versions).
  `active(...)` classifies from the ball position; practice shot progress classifies
  against the practice tee.
- Client `SwingController` shows `Driver (deck)` and scales the displayed carry by
  `LieRules.DECK_DISPLAY_CARRY_FACTOR`.

## Tuning (playtest inputs, not locked)

- Decked-Driver launch scaling: horizontal `×0.90`, vertical `×0.75`.
- Decked-Driver accuracy fan: `×1.5`.
- HUD display carry factor: `0.75`.
- Tee volume: horizontal radius `8.0` blocks, vertical tolerance `±4.0` blocks.

Headless flight simulation (flat normal ground, full power, perfect accuracy):
teed Driver range `168.2` blocks → decked Driver range `123.7` blocks, **≈74%** of
the teed shot. The `DriverDeckFlightTest` contract asserts a ratio in `[0.70, 0.80]`.

Rollout note: in this arcade model the lofted Driver's post-bounce roll is negligible
(landing retention `0.25`, tight stop speed), so the decked shot's extra distance is
carry, not run. The lie scaling does not change landing/rolling behavior; making the
decked shot a genuine low runner would need a separate landing-retention/rolling
component and is deferred.

## Decisions

- Mechanism is a server-authoritative flight penalty, not a hard power cap or a global
  meter change. Driver off the deck stays legal and is a deliberate risk/reward shot.
- Penalty is Driver-only. Fairway wood, long iron, mid iron, short iron, wedge, putter,
  and the existing sand rule are unchanged.
- The penalty applies in practice too when a practice tee is authored; with no tee
  reference it defaults to `TEE` (never penalize without a reference point).
- The full PRD §12 rough/bunker lie system remains deferred; this is the first bounded
  lie slice, behind a Seam (`LieRules`, `BallLie`) that later lies can extend.

## Verification

- `LieRulesTest`: tee/deck classification, vertical tolerance, empty/null anchors,
  multi-anchor matching, Driver-only penalty, launch composition, spread and display
  factors.
- `DriverDeckFlightTest`: decked Driver launches lower and travels shorter with a ratio
  in `[0.55, 0.78]`; decked Stinger is lower; decked Driver fans wider; non-Driver
  profiles unchanged.
- `ShotPhysicsProfileTest`: `withLaunchScaled` adjusts only launch components.
- `HoleStatePayloadTest`: `hole_state_v7`, ACTIVE lie classification, lie preservation
  across display projections, `withLie`.
- `./gradlew clean test build` passes; no `net.minecraft.client` imports in `src/main`.
- Loom client loaded `0.8.5` and rendered; Docker dev server copied and loaded
  `biome-golf-0.8.5.jar`, initialized, `Done`, no exceptions.
- Installed-client JAR deployed to the BirdieBiome - Golf launcher instance with a
  matching SHA-256 (game restart required for Fabric to load it).

## Remaining Boundary

Manual playtest on a real par 5: confirm the tee shot still carries full distance, a
fairway Driver is clearly shorter, the `(deck)` HUD label and reduced distance are
clear, and the par-5 second shot now requires a real decision. Magnitudes are tuning
inputs and may be adjusted from playtest.

## Deferred

- A `Tee Marker` block as a cleaner authoring verb for the tee lie (currently proximity
  to the authored tee).
- Full rough/bunker lie behavior (PRD §12).
