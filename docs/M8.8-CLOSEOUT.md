# M8.8 - Distance Display Units: Closeout

**Status:** Complete.

M8.8 adds golf-style yard display on top of the existing block-based model. Blocks remain
the authoritative internal unit for gameplay, networking, physics, scoring, and course
geometry; the client converts canonical block values only when rendering UI. It also
exposes server-authoritative cumulative horizontal travel for the current shot.

M8.8 was explicitly authorized as an independent presentation slice before M8.7 closeout.
M8.7's scope was unchanged.

## Delivered

- `DistanceUnit` (Minecraft-free) owns the fixed presentation conversion and rounding:
  `yards = Math.round(blocks * 1.75)`, with `BLOCKS` as the identity unit.
- `DistanceDisplayState` is client-local presentation preference, defaulting to `YARDS`.
  It is never gameplay state and is never sent to the server.
- The configurable `Y` keybind (`key.minecraft_golf.distance_units`) toggles all distance
  displays between yards and blocks, is registered in the Minecraft Golf controls
  category, and reports `Distance units: Yards|Blocks` via the overlay message.
- The Swing HUD renders the selected club's nominal distance through the active unit
  (`~263 yards` / `~150 blocks`). The putter value is labeled `roll` rather than carry.
- The Hole HUD renders the authoritative cup distance through the active unit
  (`Cup: ^ 88 yards` / `Cup: ^ 50 blocks`).
- The Hole HUD renders server-authoritative current-shot travel (`Shot: 150 yards`) from
  `HoleStatePayload.shotDistanceBlocks`, both while the ball is moving and after natural
  rest, including the practice-ball phase.
- `ShotDistanceTracker` accumulates horizontal travel per authoritative physics segment,
  ignores flight height, resets on launch and on tee/recovery repositioning, and is
  persisted on the ball entity (`golf_shot_distance`).
- `ActiveHoleService` publishes the accumulated distance on active snapshots and
  practice shot progress.
- `hole_state` was versioned to `hole_state_v6` to carry `shotDistanceBlocks`;
  `distanceToCupBlocks` remains the canonical network field.

## Display Conversion

`1 block = 1.75 yards`, rounded to the nearest whole unit.

| Club | Internal blocks | Display yards |
|---|---:|---:|
| Driver | 150 | 263 |
| Fairway Wood | 125 | 219 |
| Long Iron | 100 | 175 |
| Mid Iron | 85 | 149 |
| Short Iron | 60 | 105 |
| Wedge | 42 | 74 |
| Putter | 22 | 39 (roll) |

## Server-First Boundary

- `HoleStatePayload.distanceToCupBlocks` and `shotDistanceBlocks` are transmitted in
  blocks; the client converts only at render time.
- Physics, club tuning, collision, scoring, tap-in rules, and course geometry are
  unchanged and remain block-based.
- The server does not trust, store, or use the client's selected display unit.
- No serverbound unit preference or client-authored distance was introduced.

## Manual Verification

Automated, Loom, and Docker verification pass. The visible-HUD boundary is confirmed by
rendering, not by the automated suite. The installed launcher JAR was updated and
matches the build; a full client restart is required before the in-game check. The
recommended confirmation pass is:

- Swing HUD club distance renders in yards by default and matches the club table.
- Hole HUD cup distance renders in yards by default.
- Hole HUD current-shot travel updates while the ball is moving and settles after rest.
- `Y` toggles both HUDs between yards and blocks and is rebindable in Controls.
- Putter distance is labeled as roll.
- Solo and Ready Golf clients render the same authoritative values with no serverbound
  unit traffic.

## Deferred

- The local display-unit preference is not persisted across client restarts; it
  initializes to Yards.
- Meters, feet, and other unit systems remain out of scope.
- Server-side unit configuration, network migration from blocks to yards, and
  Bedrock/Geyser presentation remain out of scope.
