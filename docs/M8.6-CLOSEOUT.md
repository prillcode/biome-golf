# M8.6 - Concurrent Rounds, Join Clarity, and Scoring UX: Closeout

**Status:** Complete.

M8.6 delivered concurrent isolated Ready Golf rounds, complete vanilla-compatible
lobby discovery and lifecycle commands, clearer scoring presentation, and protection
and reconnect behavior that remains scoped to each player and round. M9 remains
deferred until an alternate game mode is explicitly prioritized.

## Delivered

- Multiple Ready Golf lobbies and playing rounds can coexist, including on the same
  course and hole, without suppressing unrelated solo play.
- Round membership is server-authoritative, player-scoped, and indexed by round UUID;
  stale actions and late joins are revalidated by the server.
- `/golf round list` exposes every open lobby through vanilla chat with clickable and
  visible UUID-targeted join commands. Bare `/golf round join` joins the sole open
  lobby and directs players to list choices when there are multiple lobbies.
- Optional browser, lobby, HUD, scoring, and scorecard payloads consume the same
  server-owned projections and explicit versioned schemas.
- Accepted shots are authoritative and remain distinct from penalties and Pick Up
  totals. Active cumulative relative-to-par excludes the current incomplete hole.
- Variable-length authored courses render explicit hole numbers in scorecards.
- Finalized-course protection uses an immutable dimension-keyed index covering all
  finalized authored courses and supported development-course protection.
- Completed-solo reconnect reconstructs from retained player course state, and
  round-specific disconnect, transition, result, and cleanup paths do not cross-talk.
