# M8.15 Closeout — Golf, World, and Builder Player Modes

## Delivered

Server-owned player modes for supported Java players on a trusted family server:

- **Golf** — Survival game type plus Creative-style flight and damage protection
  (`mayfly`, `invulnerable`), sharing the normal Survival inventory. Flight-only, not
  literal Creative; new players default to Golf.
- **World** — ordinary Survival/Peaceful world play, including mining and building
  outside protected course landscapes.
- **Builder** — operator-only **true Creative** (approved pivot, 2026-09-30):
  instant break/place, flight, and the full item catalogue. The curated
  `config/minecraft_golf/builder_palette.json` fills the inventory as a **palette
  starter kit** on entry, reconnect, and `/golf builder restock` — one of each of the
  30 configured landscaping blocks plus the Driver-through-Wedge club set for
  mid-build play-testing. `/golf builder palette reload` redefines the kit and
  validates the whole replacement before adoption.
- Course selection (`/golf builder course <id>`) is an informational reference; the
  authored landscape perimeter (M8.10) still protects every course from everyone, and
  a locked perimeter denies even the Creative Builder. No perimeter is required to
  enter Builder, supporting the build-holes-first workflow.
- Modes persist world-scoped per player
  (`data/minecraft_golf_player_modes.json`), carry across reconnect/respawn, and
  visitors never receive Golf/Builder privileges.
- Practice balls always play the full tee profile, so a Builder can measure hole
  lengths anywhere; the off-tee Driver penalty still applies to scored golf.
- Practice balls owned by a player are cleared automatically on disconnect, so a
  course never accumulates another player's left-behind balls.

Superseded within the milestone: the original isolated Builder (separate
inventory, perimeter-scoped edits, export blocking, per-second auto-refill, one-click
erase hook). Legacy World-inventory snapshots from that era are restored once and
retired. Golf's flight-only decision is likewise the resolved product choice; M9
alternate game modes remain deferred.

## Product Decisions (recorded)

- Flight-only Golf over literal Creative for golf play (2026-09-30); Builder is
  literal Creative because course authoring is the operator's deliberate Creative
  activity.
- Creative item crossover into World play is an accepted, trusted-server tradeoff;
  no snapshot/restore on Builder exit.
- Palette starter kit over unrestricted-everything in hand: the config file still
  defines the go-to blocks, one of each, with the 6-club play-test set.
- Erase-permission note: in Creative Builder, left-click insta-break is native; no
  custom eraser or per-second sanitation runs.
