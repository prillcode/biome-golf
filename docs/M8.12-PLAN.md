# M8.12 — Off-Tee Driver Discipline (Lie-Aware Shot Context)

**Status:** Implemented in 0.8.5. See `docs/M8.12-CLOSEOUT.md` for verification, tuning,
and the remaining manual par-5 playtest boundary. Playtest-gated magnitudes.

This milestone introduces the first bounded slice of lie-aware shot context: a Driver hit
from anywhere other than a tee box must not behave like a teed Driver. The specific target
is the "driver off the deck" exploit where long par 5s are reached with a free full-power
bomb from the fairway.

It is intentionally narrow. The full PRD §12 rough/bunker lie system remains deferred;
this milestone adds only the tee-vs-deck distinction needed to fix the exploit, behind a
seam that later lie work can extend.

## Problem

Current behavior (`ShotService.attempt`):

- The only Driver restriction is `DRIVER_NOT_ALLOWED_ON_SAND` when the ball rests on sand.
- On fairway, generic ground, ice, or any non-sand surface, a Driver launches with its full
  tee profile: `fullPowerHorizontalSpeed = 4.2524`, `fullPowerUpwardSpeed = 1.4042`,
  `LOFTED_CLUB` landing, nominal carry `150`.
- Nothing in the shot path knows whether the ball is on a tee or on the deck. The catalog
  has no tee concept; `HoleDefinition.tee()` is only used to place the ball and to protect
  blocks.

Result: club selection is not meaningfully punished for using the longest club from the
worst lie. A par 5 can be reached with a Driver tee shot plus a Driver fairway bomb at full
power, which removes the strategic reason the driver exists only on the tee.

This is within architectural intent: `ARCHITECTURE.md` §8.3 already lists "current ball
lie" as a shot-resolution input, and §11 explicitly says "Later versions can activate
lie-based shot modifiers." This milestone takes the first, bounded step.

## Options Considered

### Option A — Make the swing meter faster off the deck

Rejected as a primary fix. It only punishes mistimed input; a player who times the meter
still gets the full tee distance, so it does not close the exploit. It also cannot be
validated server-side, because the server only receives the resulting `power`/`accuracy`.
Worth keeping only as a small optional accuracy-tightening lever (see Option D).

### Option B — No bounce-back without penalty / force first-try power and accuracy

Rejected as a global rule. The three-click meter's bounce is the mechanic's forgiveness, and
the PRD requires it to stay understandable to younger/casual players ("Simple but
skill-based", §3.3; success criterion 3, §34). Forcing first-try execution would make every
shot a reflex test and contradict the M3 contract. A metered version of this could be a
future optional mode, not a fix for one club/lie.

### Option C — Hard-cap Driver power away from the tee

Rejected as the mechanism. It is blunt, removes player agency, and reads as an arbitrary
rule rather than golf. It also produces no interesting risk/reward decision; the player
cannot deliberately "go for it." A visible effective-ceiling in the HUD is acceptable, but
the underlying model should be a flight penalty, not a hidden input cap.

### Option D — Server-authoritative lie context with a decked-driver penalty (recommended)

A Driver off the deck should launch lower, carry shorter, land hotter, and be slightly less
accurate, while remaining a legal risk/reward shot. The tee remains the only place the
Driver reaches its nominal `150` carry. This:

- physically closes the par-5 exploit without removing player choice,
- reuses the existing `ShotPhysicsProfile` and `ShotResolver` seams (the same knobs
  Stinger/Chip/Flop already use),
- stays fully server-authoritative, and
- leaves a clean seam for future lies (rough, bunker) without building them now.

If playtesting shows the physical penalty is too subtle, the optional refinement is to
tighten the decked driver's accuracy window (narrower perfect band or faster accuracy
sweep) — Option A used narrowly, never globally.

## Design Direction

### Lie model (pure, Minecraft-free)

Add a small `BallLie` / `LieContext` type describing a lie's shot modifiers and HUD label:

- initial set: `TEE` and `DECK` (decked Driver). Keep the enum/seam open for later
  `ROUGH`/`BUNKER` without implementing them.
- carries: launch horizontal/vertical multipliers, landing retention, rolling-friction
  multiplier (reusing `ShotPhysicsProfile` semantics), an accuracy-spread multiplier, and a
  display label.

A pure resolver maps `(ball position, hole tee, practice tee, proximity config)` to a lie:
inside a tee volume → `TEE`, otherwise → `DECK` for the Driver. This is unit-testable with
no Minecraft types.

### Resolution

In `ShotService` (the single launch authority):

```text
effectiveProfile = shotType.profile(club)  composed with  lie.profileModifiers()
accuracy         = resolver miss remap  scaled by  lie.accuracySpreadMultiplier()
```

Add a `compose`/`multipliedBy` operation to `ShotPhysicsProfile` so lie modifiers stack
correctly on top of the chosen shot type (for example, a decked Stinger is lower than a
teed Stinger). Non-Driver clubs resolve unchanged.

Starting tuning targets for the decked Driver (playtest inputs, not locked constants):

- launch vertical `~0.35–0.45` of the tee shot → materially lower apex and carry,
- launch horizontal `~0.88–0.95` → modest speed loss,
- landing retention `~0.30–0.40`, rolling friction `~0.75–0.80` → hotter, shorter roll,
- accuracy spread `~1.5×` → harder, without touching the meter.

Target: decked Driver total distance lands meaningfully below the `150`-block nominal carry
so a long par 5 still demands a real second-shot decision.

### Tee-box definition

Recommendation: authored tee anchor + a dedicated gameplay radius and vertical tolerance
(new `teeLieRadius`, default on the order of 8 blocks, separate from the protection
`vicinityRadius` because it is a gameplay, not block-guard, value). No new block is
required and it works for authored courses today. It must also cover the practice-range tee.

A `Tee Marker` block (PRD §13) that grants the tee lie is a cleaner long-term authoring
verb and can replace proximity later; it is out of scope here.

### HUD disclosure

The player must understand why the Driver suddenly travels shorter. Options:

- club label suffix, e.g. `Driver (deck)`, and/or
- a state line such as `Deck lie — reduced flight`, and
- ideally an effective-carry estimate rather than the raw `150`.

Because the server owns the lie, prefer publishing the player's current resting-ball lie in
the existing hole-state projection and having the HUD render it, rather than duplicating the
tee math on the client. A provisional client-side estimate is acceptable only if a payload
change is undesirable.

## Locked Contracts

- The client never sends the lie; the server derives it from ball state and course metadata.
- The server remains the single shot authority and the single place a lie is applied.
- Accepted-shot counting, penalties, Pick Up, hole completion, and scorecards are unchanged.
- Player choice is preserved: Driver off the deck is legal, just penalized.
- Fairway wood and long iron are unaffected (they are deck clubs by design).
- The existing `DRIVER_NOT_ALLOWED_ON_SAND` rule stays.
- Solo and Ready Golf share the same resolution path.

## Bounded Slices

### S0 — Lie Model and Baseline

- Add the pure lie type and tee-volume resolver with tests.
- Record current teed vs. decked Driver test baselines (apex, carry, range) before changes.

### S1 — Server Resolution

- Compute the lie in `ShotService`, compose it onto the shot-type profile, and scale the
  accuracy spread.
- Verify: decked Driver is lower and shorter than teed; non-Driver clubs are byte-identical
  to today; decked Stinger stacks correctly; sand rule unchanged.
- Add `ShotPhysicsProfile` composition tests.

### S2 — HUD Disclosure

- Publish the resting-ball lie (or expose it through an existing projection) and surface
  `(deck)` / reduced-flight feedback plus an effective-carry estimate.
- Confirm no client class leaks into `src/main`.

### S3 — Calibration and Playtest

- Tune magnitudes on a real par 5 in Loom/Docker until the decked Driver is clearly
  penalized but still a viable deliberate choice.
- Confirm the par 5 can no longer be reached with a free fairway Driver bomb.
- Decide (playtest-gated) whether to add the optional decked-driver accuracy tightening.
- Record tuning and closeout.

## Non-Goals

- Full lie system: rough, bunker, greens, or per-surface shot penalties beyond today's
  surface behavior.
- Any change to the three-click input model or global swing difficulty.
- Multiple tee boxes, new clubs, Tee Marker block, or new course authoring.
- Wind, spin, shot shaping, prediction, camera, scoring, or game modes.
- A hard power cap or client-authored outcomes.

## Open Decisions

1. Mechanism: physical flight penalty (recommended) vs. hard power cap vs. meter tightening.
2. Penalty magnitude and whether `accuracySpreadMultiplier` belongs in `ShotPhysicsProfile`
   or a separate `LieContext`.
3. Tee definition: authored tee radius (recommended, no new block) vs. `Tee Marker` block.
4. HUD: club suffix vs. state line; show effective carry? publish lie vs. client estimate?
5. Practice range: apply the deck penalty consistently (recommended, and let players
   practice the shot) vs. sandbox full power everywhere.
6. Whether the optional decked-driver accuracy tightening is in this milestone or deferred.
7. Interaction with re-tees and balls that later come to rest inside a tee box.

## Exit Criteria

M8.12 is complete when:

1. A Driver from a tee box keeps its current full-power flight.
2. A Driver from a non-tee lie launches materially lower and travels materially shorter,
   with a modest accuracy penalty, as a deliberate risk/reward shot.
3. The par-5 exploit is closed: a long par 5 cannot be reached with a free full-power
   fairway Driver.
4. Fairway wood, long iron, and all other clubs are unchanged.
5. The lie is derived server-side and cannot be dictated by the client.
6. The HUD communicates the lie and reduced flight without implying a bug.
7. Existing scoring, ownership, penalties, sand rules, solo/Ready Golf, and lifecycle
   behavior are intact.
8. Automated, Loom, Docker, installed-client, and family/LAN playtest verification passes.
