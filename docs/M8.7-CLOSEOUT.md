# M8.7 - Shot Variety and Trajectory Control: Closeout

**Status:** Complete.

M8.7 adds server-authoritative trajectory choice (Standard, Chip, Stinger, and Flop) and
a server-authoritative near-cup Tap-In convenience action. Golf scoring, the round
lifecycle, and the single server-authoritative shot model are unchanged. The Standard
shot remains the default and preserves prior play behavior. M9 — Additional Game Modes
remains deferred until an alternate mode is explicitly prioritized.

## Delivered

- `ShotType` (Minecraft-free) is the server-owned intent and contextual-choice model:
  `STANDARD`, `CHIP`, `STINGER`, `FLOP`.
- The server validates the requested type against the held club before launch and
  returns `INVALID_SHOT_TYPE` for illegal combinations without consuming a scoring shot.
- `ShotResolver` resolves every type through one path with an explicit
  `ShotPhysicsProfile`; the client never sends a trajectory, landing point, distance, or
  score.
- `ShotRequestPayload` carries the requested type under the versioned identifier
  `shot_request_v2`. `ShotType.fromWire` falls back to `STANDARD` for stale/malformed
  values.
- `hole_state` was versioned to `hole_state_v5` to publish display-safe
  `tapInAvailable` state.
- The configurable `C` keybind cycles the legal contextual choices for the held club and
  the swing HUD shows the active mode (`Shot: Standard`, `Chip`, `Stinger`, `Flop`).
- Tap-In is a shared service operation (`ActiveHoleService.tapIn`) reachable through the
  contextual `B` keybind, the clickable chat prompt, and `/golf tapin`. It increments
  accepted shots and total strokes by exactly one, then completes the hole through the
  existing authoritative completion path. It is never a free completion.
- Tap-In eligibility is server-authoritative and uses the horizontal center-to-cup
  boundary in `TapInRules.withinTapInRadius` (`dx² + dz² <= 4.0`, inclusive). The radius is
  `TapInRules.TAP_IN_RADIUS_BLOCKS`, widened from one block to two blocks in `0.5.1`
  after playtest feedback that one block felt too tight.

## Allowed Shot Matrix

| Held club | Standard | Controlled result |
|---|---:|---|
| Driver | Yes | Stinger |
| Fairway Wood | Yes | Stinger |
| Long Iron | Yes | Stinger |
| Mid Iron | Yes | Chip |
| Short Iron | Yes | Chip, Flop |
| Wedge | Yes | Chip, Flop |
| Putter | Yes | Unavailable; use Standard |

The matrix is server-owned in `ShotType.allowedTypes` and pinned by
`ShotTypeTest.serverMatrixMatchesContextualChoices`.

## Tuning Decisions

Final `ShotPhysicsProfile` constants (launch horizontal, launch vertical, landing
retention, rolling friction multiplier):

| Type | Launch H | Launch V | Landing retention | Rolling friction |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Putter Standard | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 |
| Lofted Standard | 1.00 | 1.00 | 0.25 | 0.82 |
| Chip | 1.05 | 0.62 | 0.72 | 0.72 |
| Stinger | 1.08 | 0.42 | 0.60 | 0.65 |
| Flop | 0.62 | 1.55 | 0.12 | 0.50 |

Stinger rolling retention was reduced from `0.90` to `0.60` and its rolling friction
from `0.90` to `0.65` after observation that driver Stingers rolled out too far after
landing; its launch shape is unchanged. Recorded as release `0.3.3` in `CHANGELOG.md`.

**Later retune (2026-09):** stinger launch-vertical was raised from `0.42` to `0.55` so
long-iron, fairway-wood, and driver stingers fly higher and carry better. Landing retention
and rolling friction are unchanged; the stinger remains flatter than a chip and well below
standard loft, so the "low flight" identity is preserved.

## Manual Playtest

Automated, Loom, and Docker verification pass. The gameplay-feel boundary (trajectory
arcs, rollout, low-canopy clearance, tap-in at the inside/outside boundary, and
solo/Ready Golf scorecard isolation) is verified by play, not by the automated suite.

The observed Stinger roll-out tuning (recorded as `0.3.3`) is prior gameplay evidence
that calibration occurred, but a final confirmation pass on the shipped artifact is the
recommended acceptance step. The checklist is:

- Standard shots are unchanged for every club.
- Chip is lower and rolls farther than Standard for mid iron, short iron, and wedge.
- Stinger has a lower apex and clears tested low canopy for long iron, fairway wood, and
  driver.
- Flop is higher, shorter, and rolls less for short iron and wedge.
- `C` cycles the contextual choices and the HUD label tracks the held club.
- Tap-In inside the two-block boundary adds exactly one stroke and completes the hole;
  outside the boundary it is unavailable, and repeated requests are harmless.
- Solo and concurrent Ready Golf remain isolated with correct scorecards, penalties,
  Pick Up, reconnect, and replay.

## Deferred

- Additional game modes (Scramble, Match Play, Best Ball) remain M9 scope.
- Wind, lie-based effects, spin control, shot shaping, and a landing predictor remain
  out of scope.
- The client-light boundary is unchanged: custom entities, clubs, swing input, camera,
  and HUD still require the modded client.
